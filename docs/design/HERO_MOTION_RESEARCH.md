## Orbea — https://www.orbea.com/es-es

- **Opening:** “The Yes Machine” leads with a distinct campaign idea, short support copy, and two explicit next actions.
- **Scroll:** The available page view progresses from brand idea to an editorial statement, then into a visual bike-discovery sequence and riding categories. Live scroll behavior was unavailable.
- **Typography:** Compact campaign title and short, forceful product names. The hero’s title carries the identity rather than a generic ecommerce claim.
- **Image/object:** Multiple named bike families are staged as discovery moments. The page is product-led rather than UI-led.
- **Depth/parallax:** Not verifiable from the document view.
- **Transition:** Visible content order suggests a handoff from campaign to product stories to category exploration; exact masking and continuity were not verifiable.
- **Interaction:** “Discover” and “Configure” actions frame a clear next step.
- **Useful for ShopSmart:** Give the shopping journey one memorable thesis, then let real products embody it.
- **Do not copy:** The campaign phrase, cycling imagery, product-specific compositions, or Orbea’s brand language.

## Alethia — https://www.alethia.earth/

- **Opening:** A concise scientific promise is paired with a “Scroll to discover” cue and a visible impact metric in the first experience.
- **Scroll:** The available page view orders the hero metric, numbered capability statements, then larger solution and technology chapters. Live scroll behavior was unavailable.
- **Typography:** A large thesis is supported by short, numbered explanations; the hierarchy moves from headline to evidence.
- **Image/object:** The hero includes layered visual/data content and an emissions metric before the explanatory content.
- **Depth/parallax:** Not verifiable from the document view.
- **Transition:** The story moves from a high-level claim into numbered proof, then into deeper capabilities.
- **Interaction:** “Scroll to discover” makes the continuation legible; the metric gives the opening a concrete focal point.
- **Useful for ShopSmart:** Make the recommendation’s evidence visible as part of the reveal, rather than asking shoppers to trust an unexplained label.
- **Do not copy:** Alethia’s environmental metrics, scientific claims, imagery, or visual identity.

## ShopSmart direction

Use a single pinned, scroll-progress-driven scene that changes its composition as the shopper moves from intent to shortlist, comparison, evidence, and cart. Let backend products and their existing photography carry the story; reserve the strongest depth and scale shift for the evidence-backed best fit. Keep the transition into the merchandise rail continuous. This is an original direction informed by the references’ visible emphasis on campaign thesis, object-led imagery, and claim-to-evidence progression; direct interaction-level comparison remains unverified until a browser surface is available.
